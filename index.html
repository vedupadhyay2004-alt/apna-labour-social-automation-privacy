Privacy Policy

Social Automation Platform · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Apna Labour (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when Apna Labour staff use Social Automation Platform, our self-hosted Postiz service for preparing, scheduling, and publishing content on Apna Labour’s social-media accounts.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use the service, information may include your account name and email address; connected social-account identifiers, usernames, profile details, and authorization credentials; post text, uploaded media, and scheduled publishing times; and technical information recorded by the server or its hosting provider, such as request time, IP address, and error details.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, connect the social accounts you choose, prepare and schedule posts, publish content at your direction, show account and publishing status, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service.

Social platforms and service providers

When you connect an account or publish content, the relevant social platform receives information needed to authenticate the connection and carry out your request. Its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy. The service is self-hosted. The current installation runs on Apna Labour’s computer using Docker, with its database and uploaded media stored in local Docker-managed storage. The operator’s device and Docker setup control access to that information. If the service is moved to another host or storage provider, this policy should be updated.

Storage and security

Information is stored in the service’s configured database and media storage. Access credentials are stored by the service so it can perform actions you request. The operator should restrict server and database access, protect secrets, and use appropriate network and backup safeguards. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Connected-account information and content remain in the service until the operator removes them or deletes the relevant account or installation. You can disconnect social accounts and delete content through the service where those controls are available. No automated application backup schedule is configured in the current Docker setup; any device-level backups depend on the operator’s computer settings. You can also revoke the service’s access from the connected social platform.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the social platforms and infrastructure providers needed to operate the service, or when required to protect the service, comply with law, or respond to a valid legal request.

Choices and requests

You may disconnect an account, remove content, or ask questions about access, correction, or deletion by contacting surakshacharmi1122@gmail.com. Social-platform permissions can also be reviewed or revoked in that platform’s settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. We will post the updated version here and revise the date above.
